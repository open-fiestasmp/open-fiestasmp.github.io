Server Update

Transitioning to Semi-No-Rules Survival

Effective November 10, 2026

FiestaSMP is shifting toward a semi-no-rules survival server. Base raiding, stealing, and PvP are part of survival now. Claims no longer prevent base raiding or roll back griefing: if you want to keep your items safe, travel deep into the wilderness, hide your chests underground, and keep your coordinates secret.

What is Allowed
  • Base Raiding & Griefing: Raiding bases and breaking in for loot is fair game.
  • Stealing & Looting: Any chests or containers you find in the wild can be looted.
  • Open World PvP: Combat is enabled everywhere outside of spawn docks.
Zero Tolerance Bans
  • Identity Slurs & Severe Abuse: The n-word and racial or gender slurs bring an instant permanent ban. (Casual swearing is allowed with in-game warnings, but slurs are zero tolerance).
  • Doxxing & Real-Life Threats: No IP grabbing, DDoS, or real-life drama (don't bring in someone's family or school).
  • Hacks & Cheats: X-ray, killaura, fly, speed, auto-clickers, and Baritone bots.
  • Lag Machines & Duping: Intentional server crashers and item duplication exploits.
Zero-tolerance offenses result in an immediate permanent ban without warnings. Read full rules & guidelines ›
Trading & Economy

Real Diamonds & Barter

No virtual server money, no bought coins, and no pay-to-win kits. Players trade directly with physical diamonds, gold, and barter items using /trade <player> or chest shops.

Account Setup

GUI Login & Custom Skins

When you first connect, follow the in-game GUI authentication prompt to secure your username. We use an interactive GUI menu instead of chat commands to keep spam bots out. Set your custom skin anytime with /skin <username>.
