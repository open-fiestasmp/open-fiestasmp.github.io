Season 1 Ending & 46-Day Reset Notice

Posted on September 21, 2025 by debarkak

After running continuously since April 2019—a run of 6 years and 5 months—Season 1 of FiestaSMP is finally coming to an end.

We will be resetting the server and launching Season 2 in 46 days, on November 6, 2025.

The 500GB Map & World Downloads

Over the past six-plus years, thousands of chunks have been generated and explored across the world. The server map currently sits at over 500GB in size.

Because of this massive size, we unfortunately cannot back up the full world folder or provide it as a public world download. Our hosting and bandwidth simply cannot handle distributing half a terabyte of world files. We know many of you spent months or years on your builds, and we are sorry that we cannot offer an archive download for this map.

What You Should Do Before November 6th

You have 46 days before the world is wiped clean. During this time:

  1. Take Screenshots & Clips: Press F1 in-game and take plenty of screenshots or video walkthroughs of your bases, farms, redstone contraptions, and favorite spots.
  2. Share Your Builds: Post your favorite screenshots in #media on our Discord or send them through the Support Form so we can preserve them in the website Gallery.
  3. Hang Out: Spend these final weeks finishing any projects you want to see completed or visiting other players’ builds across the map.

Thank You

Running Season 1 for 6 years and 5 months has been an incredible journey. Thank you to everyone who built bases, traded, fought, and called FiestaSMP home since 2019.

We will see you all on a fresh map on November 6th for Season 2!
