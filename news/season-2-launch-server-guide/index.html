Season 2 Launch & Map Reset

Posted on November 6, 2025 by debarkak

Season 2 has officially begun! The map reset is complete and the brand new world is live.

Everyone starts with an empty inventory on a completely fresh map. Here is how to join and get started:

Server Address & Ports

You can join on both Java and Bedrock editions:

  • Server Address: fiesta.debarkak.xyz
  • Java Port: 25565 (default)
  • Bedrock Port: 19132 (default)
  • Accounts: Both premium and offline-mode accounts are supported.

First Login (In-Game GUI)

When you join the server for the first time, follow the in-game GUI prompt on your screen to secure your account. The GUI method helps stop spam bots from affecting the server’s player count.

Starting Out

Grab some starter food at the spawn docks, craft a boat, and head out into the wilderness to find a spot for your base.

If you run into any issues or want to chat with the community, hop into our Discord.
