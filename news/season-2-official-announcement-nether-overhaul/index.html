Season 2 Official Announcement & Nether Overhaul

debarkak FiestaSMP Core Team
November 6, 2026
2 min read

Welcome to FiestaSMP Season 2! Over the past few weeks, our core team and community contributors have worked tirelessly to prepare an expanded and refined survival multiplayer experience.

What is New in Season 2?

Here is an overview of the major features and systems introduced for this season:

1. The Great Nether Highway

To facilitate trade and travel across long distances, we have constructed the primary Nether Highway hub at coordinates (0, 70, 0) in the Nether dimension:

  • Packed ice boat express routes connecting all four cardinal directions.
  • Designated community portals linking Spawn Archipelago directly to distant player outposts.
  • Protected corridor guidelines to prevent portal conflicts and unauthorized obstructions.

2. Economy & Merchant Trade Rebalance

We gathered extensive feedback throughout Season 1 regarding currency flow and trading balance. Key updates include:

  • Reduced diamond inflation by introducing server trade sinks (rare cosmetic blocks, custom titles).
  • Structured villager trade adjustments to preserve survival progression.
  • Secure community trading stalls at Spawn Harbor.

3. Crossplay & Connection Stability

Our underlying Folia server architecture has been upgraded to improve tick stability and Bedrock parity:

  • Java Edition Address: fiesta.debarkak.xyz:25565
  • Bedrock Edition Address: fiesta.debarkak.xyz:19132

Server Reminder: PvP, griefing of unprotected structures, and competitive raiding are permitted according to our rules. Check /terms.html for full conduct guidelines.

See you all in-game!

— debarkak (FiestaSMP Owner)
