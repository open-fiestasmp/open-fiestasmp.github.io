Spawn Docks & Screenshot Submissions

Posted on October 18, 2026 by cbladeOfficial & debarkak

debarkak and I finished setting up the spawn harbor docks to help everyone get moving when they first log in. Just a quick reminder: there is no spawn protection radius on FiestaSMP, so grab some starter food, set sail, and claim your land to stay safe once you pick a spot.

Spawn Docks

  • Boat Slipways: Wooden docks where you can place boats and sail out across the ocean.
  • Starter Crops: Public wheat, carrot, and potato patches for quick food. Replant whatever you harvest so the next person doesn’t starve.
  • Nether Access: Public stairway going up to the Nether portal to make traveling long distances easier.
