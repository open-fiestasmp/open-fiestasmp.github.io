FiestaSMP × Celunah Partnership

Posted on November 13, 2026 by gautam652

hey everyone!

we’re partnering with celunah to help improve FiestaSMP.

as part of this, he’s gonna provide us with a Contabo VPS in the USA. we’re planning to use it as a proxy, so players joining from different countries can hopefully have a smoother connection. it’ll also help us add another layer of security to the server.

celunah will be working with us on the server itself too, helping us improve security and fix things that have been causing interruptions. we know it’s been annoying when stuff goes wrong while everyone’s trying to play, so we’re putting some work into making fiesta more reliable.

it’s gonna take a bit of time to get everything set up and tested properly. we’ll keep you guys updated, and once the project is finished, we’ll make another official announcement explaining what we’ve done.

thanks to celunah for helping us with this, and thanks to everyone for sticking with fiesta ❤️
