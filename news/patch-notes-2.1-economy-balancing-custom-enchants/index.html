Patch Notes 2.1: Economy Balancing & Custom Enchants

debarkak FiestaSMP Core Team
October 28, 2026
1 min read

Welcome to the Patch 2.1 release notes for FiestaSMP. This update focuses on economy stability, combat logging safeguards, and server responsiveness.

Balance & Gameplay Changes

Combat Logging & Safety

  • Combat Tag Duration: Increased active combat timer from 15 seconds to 25 seconds upon engaging in player-versus-player combat.
  • Disconnect Penalty: Disconnecting while in active combat will result in an automatic death trigger and item drop to prevent unfair combat escapes.
  • Ender Pearl Cooldown: Adjusted ender pearl usage cooldown to 12 seconds during active engagements.

Economy & Loot Tables

  • Ancient Debris: Netherite mining yield rate slightly normalized in outer Nether chunks.
  • Fishing Loot: Corrected enchanted book drop probabilities for early-game rod setups.
  • Bounty System: Added community bounty boards where players can place diamond bounties on notorious outlaws.

Technical Optimizations

Build: Folia 1.21.x-Custom
Tick Performance: 20.0 TPS sustained across all 4 thread regions
Chunk Loading: Async multi-threaded chunk pipeline enabled

If you encounter any unexpected bugs or anomalies, please file a ticket via our Discord server or submit through the Contact & Support page.
