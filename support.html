Knowledgebase & Common Questions

INSTANT ANSWERS
Can I claim land or protect my chests? +
No. There are no claim plugins (Towny, GriefPrevention, or Factions). Fiesta SMP is survival with the safety off. Anything left unprotected is fair game. Protect your coordinates, travel thousands of blocks away, and bury your valuables.
How do Bedrock edition players join? +
Use server address fiesta.debarkak.xyz with Bedrock port 19132. Our Geyser-Floodgate bridge allows players on Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android, and Windows 10/11 to play together on the exact same server map.
Are offline / cracked clients supported? +
Yes! Open authentication is enabled. You can connect with any official or third-party client. Your player UUID and inventory remain consistent across sessions.
How does trading work without money or /pay? +
All trading is 100% physical barter. Diamonds, ancient debris, golden apples, and enchanted books are commonly used as barter commodities. Trade in person — and watch your back.
When will the server reset or wipe? +
Each season lasts between 1.5 to 3 years. We announce season finales months ahead, and complete world save downloads are provided free to all players.
Can I purchase ranks or pay-to-win items? +
Never. There are no donor ranks, no crate keys, no booster packs, and no pay-to-win kits. Every player has identical vanilla gameplay privileges.
