Starting tomorrow, **November 10, 2026**, FiestaSMP is shifting toward a **semi-no-rules** survival server.

We ran polls in Discord and talked through how we want survival to feel. The server has always had a candid, rough-around-the-edges community, and handling endless claim dispute tickets when people raid each other takes away from what makes vanilla survival fun. So we are opening up the wild:

## What is Allowed (Effective Nov 10)

- **Griefing & Base Raiding:** Raiding rival bases, breaching defenses with TNT, and claiming whatever loot you find.
- **Stealing & Looting:** Finding hidden chests, looting farms, and taking resources anywhere in the wild.
- **Open-World PvP:** Combat is enabled across the map outside of spawn docks—ambushes, bounties, and team skirmishes.
- **Free Barter & Diamond Economy:** Unrestricted player-to-player trade with physical diamonds and barter without artificial server taxes.
- **Alliances & Clan Rivalries:** Teaming up with friends, building secret shared bases, and settling rivalries in-game.
- **Performance & Visual Mods:** Client-side optimization mods like Sodium, Iris shaders, Lithium, OptiFine, and clean HUDs.

### How to Keep Your Stuff Safe

Because claims no longer prevent raiding or roll back griefing, you need to rely on vanilla survival instincts:
1. **Travel far from spawn:** Don't build within sight of (0, 0) or the spawn docks. Head a few thousand blocks out.
2. **Hide your base:** Underground vaults, hidden entrances under natural terrain, and off-axis travel keep your base hidden.
3. **Guard your coordinates:** Do not stream or share your F3 screen with people you do not trust.

## What is Strictly Banned (Zero Tolerance)

Semi-no-rules means freedom inside normal survival gameplay—it does **not** mean anything goes. The following will get you **permanently banned** without a warning:

1. **Cheats & Hacked Clients:** X-ray texture packs/mods, killaura, fly hacks, speed hacks, freecam, auto-clickers, and botting scripts like Baritone. Play with standard vanilla survival mechanics.
2. **Doxxing, DDoS & Real-Life Drama:** Any IP grabbing, threats, harassment, or digging into players' real-life identities (no calling people's families, schools, or employers). Keep everything strictly in the game.
3. **Identity Slurs & Hate Speech:** Racial, ethnic, or gender-based slurs and hate speech bring an immediate permanent ban. Casual swearing out of frustration is allowed (though in-game warnings may remind you it is discouraged), but slurs and targeted hate speech are zero tolerance.
4. **Lag Machines:** Intentionally building redstone lag machines, entity crammers, or chunk loaders meant to lag or crash the server.
5. **Duping:** Item duplication glitches and exploits. Duped items are cleared and offenders are banned.
