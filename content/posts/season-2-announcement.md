FiestaSMP Season 2 is now open to all players. The world is fresh, crossplay is active, and the survival mechanics are set. Here is what you need to know to get started.

## How to Join

Both Java and Bedrock players can join using the address below:

- **Server Address:** `fiesta.debarkak.xyz`

Any type of accounts are welcome.

## Getting Started

1. **Register your account:** When you join for the first time, type `/register <password> <confirmPassword>` in chat. On future logins, simply use `/login <password>`.
2. **Set your skin:** Use `/skin <username>` if you want to load a custom skin.
3. **Leave the spawn island:** The spawn area is a safe hub. Once you travel past the 150-block perimeter, you are in open wilderness.

## Server Features

- **Wilderness Survival:** Outside of spawn, PvP and raiding are enabled. Protect your bases by building smart and keeping your coordinates safe.
- **Fair Teleports:** When using `/tpa <player>`, the other player must type `/tpaccept` to confirm. Moving or taking damage during the short warmup cancels the teleport.
- **Physical Barter Economy:** There is no virtual currency or artificial money plugins. Trade directly with other players using `/trade <player>` or set up diamond/gold chest shops with ChestShop.
- **Clans:** Team up with friends using `/team create <name>` and `/team invite <player>`.

Have fun, explore the new world, and build something great. If you need assistance or want to chat with the community, join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/6wS2dpyX57).
