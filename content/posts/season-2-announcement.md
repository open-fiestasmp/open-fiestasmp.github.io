Season 2 is officially up and running! debarkak and I have set up a brand new world, crossplay is ready to go, and there is plenty of space for everyone to build and survive.

## Joining the Server

Both Java and Bedrock players can connect directly using:

- **Java IP:** `fiesta.debarkak.xyz`
- **Bedrock IP:** `fiesta.debarkak.xyz`

Default ports apply (no port numbers needed). Any type of accounts are welcome.

## Getting Started

1. **Register:** When you first join, type `/register <password> <confirmPassword>` in chat to lock your account. On your next logins, just type `/login <password>`.
2. **Skin setup:** If your skin looks like a default Steve or Alex, use `/skin <username>` or `/skin <url>` to grab your skin.
3. **No spawn protection:** Heads up, there is no spawn protection radius here. The world is open survival with PvP and griefing enabled right from spawn, so claim your land as soon as you find a spot for your base to stay safe.

## Server Features & Rules

- **Survival & PvP:** PvP and raiding are turned on. Keep your coordinates private, lock your chests, and make sure to claim your land so nobody messes with your builds.
- **Teleporting:** If you send a `/tpa <player>`, they need to accept it with `/tpaccept`. Moving or taking hits during the short warmup cancels the teleport so people can't run away mid-fight.
- **Trading & Shops:** No fake scoreboard money or virtual economy plugins. We trade with actual items like diamonds and gold. You can trade securely with `/trade <player>` or set up chest shops.
- **Clans:** Team up with friends using `/team create <name>` and `/team invite <player>`.

## Season & Map Resets

Map resets happen whenever we feel like doing a fresh start, but we'll always give a 45-day notice before any reset happens. When the server shuts down for a reset, the world map archive will be uploaded so you can download and keep everything you built.

Jump in, invite your friends, and have fun! If you run into any issues or want to hang out with the community, hop into our [Discord](https://discord.gg/6wS2dpyX57).
