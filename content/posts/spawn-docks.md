debarkak and I finished setting up the spawn harbor area so new players have a decent starting point.

## What is at Spawn

- **Boat Ramps:** Wooden ramps where you can place boats and sail off into the ocean.
- **Starter Crops:** Public wheat, carrot, and potato patches for food. Replant what you take so people after you do not starve.
- **Nether Portal:** A stairway up to a public Nether portal to make travel easier.

Keep in mind that there is no spawn protection radius here. Grab what food you need, get away from spawn, and hide your chests once you pick a spot for your base.

## Submitting Screenshots for the Gallery

We have a [Gallery](/gallery.html) on the website where we show screenshots of player bases, builds, and world locations taken by debarkak and server players.

If you want your base on the site:
1. Press `F1` in-game to hide your hotbar and chat.
2. Take a screenshot.
3. Send it in `#media` on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/6wS2dpyX57) or submit it through our [Support Form](/contactsupport.html) with your in-game name.
