We finished setting up the spawn harbor area so new players have a decent starting point when they first join.

## What is at Spawn

- **Boat Ramps:** Wooden ramps where you can place boats and sail out into the ocean.
- **Starter Crops:** Public wheat, carrot, and potato patches for food. Replant what you take so people arriving after you do not starve.
- **Nether Portal:** A stairway up to a public Nether portal to make long-distance travel easier.

Keep in mind that there is no spawn protection radius here. Grab what food you need, get away from spawn, and hide your chests once you pick a spot for your base.

## Submitting Screenshots for the Gallery

We have an in-game [Gallery](/gallery.html) on the website where we show screenshots of player bases, builds, and world areas.

If you want your base featured on the site:
1. Press `F1` in-game to hide your hotbar and chat.
2. Take an in-game screenshot.
3. Post it in `#media` on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/6wS2dpyX57) or submit it through our [Support Form](/contactsupport.html) with your in-game username.
