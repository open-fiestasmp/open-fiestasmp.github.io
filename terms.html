Rules for survival, claims, trading, and chat.

These are the rules for playing on FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz). Most of this is common sense: play fair, don't cheat, and respect people's claims.

Note: There is no spawn protection radius. The map is open survival with PvP and raiding turned on everywhere outside of claims. Always claim your base to keep your items and builds safe.

1. Cheating & Allowed Mods

No cheats, hacks, or unfair advantages:

  • Hacks and Cheats: Modified clients (fly, speed, killaura, auto-clickers), X-ray texture packs/mods, freecam, seed finders, and botting scripts like Baritone get you permanently banned.
  • Allowed Mods: Standard client performance mods (Sodium, OptiFine, Iris), basic minimaps without entity radar or cave finders, and Litematica (with auto-place turned off). If you are unsure about a mod, ask in Discord before using it.
  • Lag Machines & Duping: Don't build lag machines or use item duplication glitches. Duped items get deleted, and anyone doing it gets banned.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting while in combat triggers an automatic death that drops your inventory.

2. Land Claims & Base Protection

How base protection works:

  • Claimed Land: Griefing or stealing inside someone's claim is not allowed. If someone manages to grief your claim, we can check block logs and roll it back.
  • Unclaimed Land: Any chest, build, or farm left unclaimed in the wild can be raided or looted by anyone. If you care about your build, claim it.
  • Griefing: Lava casts, water spam, or placing cobblestone pillars around someone else's claim will be removed and you can be punished for it.

3. Diamond Trading & Shops

No virtual money or pay-to-win:

  • Trading with Items: There is no server currency or bought ranks. Players trade directly with items, mostly diamonds and gold.
  • Safe Trading: Use /trade <player> or buy from player-owned chest shops. If you drop items on the ground in the wild to trade, you take your own risk.
  • No Real-Money Trading (RMT): Selling or buying in-game items, bases, or currency for real-world money is not allowed and will get you banned.

4. Chat & Conduct

Chat guidelines:

  • Treat People Decently: No harassment, slurs, doxxing, or personal threats in chat or Discord.
  • No Advertising: Don't paste links to other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, or personal promo in public chat.
  • No Chat Spam: Don't flood chat with repeated messages, walls of caps, or automated spam.
  • Your Account: Any type of accounts are welcome here. Whatever happens on your account name is your responsibility.

5. Map Resets

Map resets happen whenever we feel like doing a fresh start. We always give a 45-day notice before any reset so you have time to finish whatever you are building. After a season shuts down, we upload the world map archive so anyone can download it and keep their builds.

6. Reports & Appeals

Reporting problems and appeals:

  • Reports: If you run into a cheater or someone breaking rules, open a ticket on Discord with screenshots or video clips.
  • Appeals: If you think a ban or mute was unfair, you can open an appeal ticket on Discord. We usually check them within a couple of days.

FiestaSMP is an independent server and is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft. For server contact, email fiestamg@murena.io.
