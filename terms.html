Terms of Service & Rules

By joining FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz) or participating in our community channels, you agree to the following terms:

1. Gameplay & Combat

  • Allowed Mechanics: Raiding, griefing unprotected builds, theft, PvP combat, and clan warfare are fully permitted. Protect your coordinates and gear accordingly.
  • Prohibited Exploits: Hacked clients (Killaura, Fly, Speed, Baritone, Freecam, Chest-ESP), packet modifications, duplication exploits, and intentional lag machines are strictly forbidden. Violators receive an immediate permanent IP and hardware ban.
  • Allowed Client Mods: Client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris, Lithium) and Simple Voice Chat.

2. Economy & Trading

  • Pure Barter Model: There is no virtual currency, /pay command, or server shop. All trading is strictly physical player-to-player barter.
  • Trade Risks: Item exchanges, clan alliances, and meetups occur at your own risk. Staff will not reimburse items lost to scamming, betrayal, or ambushes.

3. Seasons & Resets

  • Lifecycle: FiestaSMP operates on long-term seasonal lifecycles lasting roughly 1.5 to 3 years.
  • Wipes: Full world and inventory resets happen exclusively at the launch of a new season and will be announced well in advance.

4. Community Conduct

  • Doxxing, distribution of malicious software, network disruption attempts, and extreme hate speech will result in an immediate blacklist.
  • Attempting to circumvent network penalties using alternative accounts or proxies will result in automated bans across all connected profiles.
