01 · Gameplay & Combat

Allowed Mechanics:

Raiding, griefing unprotected builds, theft, PvP combat, and clan warfare are fully permitted. Protect your coordinates and gear accordingly. Staff will not roll back griefed bases or raided chests.

Raiding Griefing unprotected builds Theft PvP combat Clan warfare

Prohibited Exploits (Permanent Ban):

Hacked clients (Killaura, Fly, Speed, Baritone, Freecam, Chest-ESP), packet modifications, duplication exploits, and intentional lag machines are strictly forbidden. Violators receive an immediate permanent IP and hardware ban.

Killaura Fly Speed Baritone Freecam Chest-ESP Packet mods Dupes Lag machines

Allowed Client Performance Mods:

Client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris, Lithium) and Simple Voice Chat for proximity audio.

Sodium Iris Lithium Simple Voice Chat
02 · Economy & Barter

Pure Barter Model:

There is no virtual currency, no /pay command, and no server shop. All trading is strictly physical player-to-player barter. Every exchange happens face-to-face.

Trade Risks Disclaimer:

Item exchanges, clan alliances, and trade meetups occur entirely at your own risk. Staff will not reimburse items lost to scamming, betrayal, ambushes, or bad deals. Choose your trading partners wisely.

Face-to-face barter Zero virtual cash No /pay Buyer beware
03 · Seasons & Resets

Long-Haul Lifecycle:

Fiesta SMP operates on long-term seasonal lifecycles lasting roughly 1.5 to 3 years. We value projects that take months or years to construct.

Wipe Policy:

Full world and inventory resets happen exclusively at the launch of a new season. Wipes are announced well in advance on Discord and YouTube, and full world download archives are published for the community to keep permanently.

1.5 – 3 years duration No sudden wipes World download provided
04 · Community Conduct

While in-game conflict, clan warfare, and trash-talking are fair game in the context of gameplay, the following actions will result in an immediate irreversible blacklist:

  • Doxxing or revealing any player or staff member's real-life personal information.
  • Distribution of malicious software, phishing links, or trojans.
  • Network disruption attempts, packet floods, or DDoS threats.
  • Extreme hate speech, severe harassment, or real-life threats.
  • Attempting to circumvent penalties using alt accounts or VPN proxies (automated cross-ban).
05 · Administration & Disclaimer

Important Notice Regarding Inquiries:

We do not accept paid sponsorships, server host promotions, or external brand deals under any circumstances.

We are also not recruiting for the core team or accepting staff applications — administration is strictly private. Any messages sent regarding sponsorships, host promotions, or joining the staff will be completely ignored and deleted without a reply.
