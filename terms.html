Connecting to FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz:25565 or Bedrock port 19132) constitutes acceptance of these operating terms & anti-cheat policies.

Perimeter Notice: Spawn island (150-block radius from 0,0) is protected by region flags. All terrain outside this perimeter is unmoderated wilderness. Keep your coordinates private & lock your containers.

1. Combat & Base Raiding

The server enforces vanilla survival mechanics with minimal moderation interventions:

  • Allowed Actions: Raiding, griefing unprotected builds, looting containers, & open-world PvP combat are permitted in all dimensions outside the spawn perimeter.
  • Combat Disconnects: Disconnecting while combat-tagged triggers an immediate player death & drops full inventory.
  • Prohibited Modifications:
    • Modified clients (Meteor, Aristois, Wurst), automated Baritone routines, & X-ray texture packs.
    • Lag machines, redstone clock overloads designed to drop tick rates below 18.0 TPS, or entity cramming over 24 entities per block.
    • Duplication exploits. Duplicated items are purged via CoreProtect database queries without restoration.

2. Physical Item Barter & Trade Rules

We don't run virtual economy plugins, scoreboard coins, or real-money stores.

  • Physical Barter: Trading uses physical items in chests or direct player trades via InTrade. Diamonds & ancient debris serve as standard units of exchange.
  • Wilderness Trade Risk: Theft during wilderness trades is treated as standard survival risk. Use the enclosed trading stalls at Spawn Harbor for protected exchanges.
  • Real-Money Transactions (RMT): Selling in-game items, base coordinates, or services for fiat currency or cryptocurrency results in an immediate permanent ban.

3. Season Durations & World Resets

Worlds remain active for multi-year periods to support large-scale redstone networks & base builds.

Reset Policy: World resets occur only when major Minecraft version updates introduce breaking terrain generation changes, or upon clan consensus. We give 30 days notice before world archival, and release world download tarballs on GitHub.

4. Chat Conduct & Account Bans

Combat & raiding are part of the game; personal abuse outside game mechanics is not:

  • Prohibited Conduct: Dox threats, IP distribution, hate speech, and spamming automated chat bots result in permanent IP & hardware ID bans.
  • Moderation Logs: Chat messages & command executions are logged for 90 days.
  • Ban Appeals: Submit appeals through the ticket system on Discord. Staff review tickets within 48 hours. Repeated pings or spamming moderators closes the appeal permanently.

5. Server Administration & Legal Notice

FiestaSMP is an independent multiplayer server. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Mojang AB or Microsoft Corporation.

For administrative notices, email fiestamg@murena.io or join our Discord server.
