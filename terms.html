Server Policy

Transitioning to Semi-No-Rules Survival (Effective Nov 10, 2026)

Effective November 10, 2026

Following Discord community polls, FiestaSMP is shifting toward a semi-no-rules survival server effective November 10, 2026. The server has always had a candid community, and handling endless claim dispute tickets when people raid each other takes away from what makes vanilla survival fun. We are opening up the wild: griefing, stealing, base raiding, and open-world PvP are allowed. Land claims no longer prevent raiding or roll back griefing.

What is Allowed
  • Griefing & Base Raiding: Raiding rival bases, breaching defenses with TNT, and claiming whatever loot you find.
  • Stealing & Looting: Finding hidden chests, looting farms, and taking resources anywhere in the wild.
  • Open-World PvP: Combat enabled across the entire map outside spawn docks—ambushes, bounties, and skirmishes.
  • Survival Economy & Free Barter: Earn balance by selling resources with /sell and /shop, trade player surplus in /auction, send funds with /pay, or barter items directly.
  • Alliances & Clan Rivalries: Teaming up with friends, building secret shared bases, and settling rivalries in-game.
  • Performance & Visual Mods: Client optimization mods (Sodium, Iris shaders, Lithium, OptiFine) and clean HUDs.
Zero Tolerance Bans
  • Cheats & Hacked Clients: X-ray texture packs/mods, killaura, fly hacks, speed hacks, freecam, auto-clickers, and Baritone bots.
  • Doxxing, DDoS & Real-Life Drama: Any IP grabbing, threats, harassment, or digging into real-life identities.
  • Identity Slurs & Hate Speech: Racial, ethnic, or gender slurs bring an immediate permanent ban without warning.
  • Lag Machines: Intentionally building redstone crashers, entity crammers, or chunk loaders.
  • Duping Glitches: Item duplication exploits (duped items cleared and offenders permanently banned).
Semi-no-rules means freedom inside normal survival gameplay—it does not mean anything goes. Violating zero-tolerance rules results in an immediate permanent ban.

1. Cheats, Hacks & Client Mods (Zero Tolerance)

Semi-no-rules provides freedom inside normal survival mechanics, but cheating and unfair client automation are strictly prohibited. The following will get you permanently banned without warning:

  • Cheats & Hacked Clients (Permanent Ban): X-ray texture packs/mods, killaura, fly hacks, speed hacks, reach, freecam, seed finders, auto-clickers, and botting scripts like Baritone. Play with standard vanilla survival mechanics.
  • Doxxing, DDoS & Real-Life Drama (Permanent Ban): Any IP grabbing, DDoS threats, harassment, or digging into players' real-life identities (no calling people's families, schools, or employers). Keep everything strictly inside the game.
  • Identity Slurs & Hate Speech (Permanent Ban): Racial, ethnic, or gender-based slurs and hate speech bring an immediate permanent ban. Casual swearing out of frustration is allowed (though automated in-game warnings remind you it is discouraged), but slurs and targeted hate speech are zero tolerance.
  • Lag Machines (Permanent Ban): Intentionally building redstone lag machines, entity crammers, or chunk loaders meant to lag or crash the server.
  • Duping Glitches (Permanent Ban): Any form of item duplication exploit. Duped inventories are cleared and all players involved are permanently banned.
  • Allowed Mods: Client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris shaders, Lithium, OptiFine), simple HUD mods, and basic minimaps (without entity radar, player tracking, or cave mapping). If you are unsure whether a mod is allowed, ask in Discord before logging in with it.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting or force-quitting during combat triggers an automatic death that drops your inventory where you stood.

2. Base Raiding, Griefing & Wilderness Survival

How survival works under our semi-no-rules direction:

  • No Protected Land Claims or Bubble: There is no protected spawn bubble or claim rollback system. Outside of the immediate starter docks, the entire map is open survival. Claims no longer prevent base raiding or roll back griefing.
  • Griefing & Base Raiding Allowed: Raiding rival bases, breaching defenses with TNT, looting chests, and taking builds is allowed. There is no rollback of raided blocks or looted items.
  • Stealing & Wild Looting: Any chests, containers, farms, or structures discovered anywhere in the wilderness are fair game to loot.
  • Open-World PvP: Combat is enabled across the map outside spawn docks—ambushes, bounties, and team skirmishes. Trust other players at your own risk.
  • Survival Economy & Free Barter: Earn balance by selling resources to the server via /sell and /shop, trade player surplus in /auction, send funds with /pay, or trade physical diamonds directly without artificial server taxes.
  • Alliances & Clan Bases: You are free to group up, coordinate defenses, establish alliances, and build secret shared bases anywhere you choose.
  • Performance & Visual Mods: Client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris shaders, Lithium, OptiFine) and clean HUDs are welcomed.
  • How to Keep Your Stuff Safe:

    Because claims no longer prevent raiding or roll back griefing, you need to rely on vanilla survival instincts:

    1. Travel far from spawn: Do not build within sight of (0, 0) or the spawn docks. Head several thousand blocks out into the wilderness.
    2. Hide your base: Underground vaults, hidden entrances disguised under natural terrain, and off-axis travel keep your base hidden.
    3. Guard your coordinates: Never stream or share your F3 debug coordinates with people you do not trust.

3. Economy, Selling & Marketplace

A fair survival economy without pay-to-win:

  • Earning Balance & Selling Items: FiestaSMP features a virtual economy ($) backed by EssentialsX. Earn money by selling gathered crops, minerals, wood, and mob drops to the server using the interactive /sell GUI, quick sell commands (/sell hand, /sell all), or category listings in /shop. Check server sell prices anytime with /worth [item].
  • Community Marketplace & Player Payments: There is no infinite server buy shop. Items sold through /sell enter the community /auction (or /ah), where players can purchase surplus gear and building resources with their earned balance. Transfer funds directly to other players using /pay <player> <amount>, check leaderboards with /baltop, or toggle your stats HUD with /hud.
  • Physical Barter: Players can also trade physical items (diamonds, netherite, gold) directly in the world. Note that automated chest shops and GUI /trade windows are disabled; all item bartering between players occurs manually.
  • No Real-Money Trading (RMT): Selling or buying in-game items, accounts, or bases for real-world money is strictly prohibited and will get both parties banned.

4. Chat, Swearing & Real-Life Conduct

We want chat to feel natural without turning into real-life toxicity:

  • Casual Swearing & In-Game Warnings: Ordinary swearing used casually or out of frustration is allowed. The in-game chat monitor may issue automated warnings reminding you that swearing is discouraged, but casual profanity alone is not a punishable offense unless it goes too far.
  • Zero Tolerance for Slurs & Hate Speech (Permanent Ban): Identity-based slurs, racial or ethnic slurs, and severe harassment are strictly prohibited under zero tolerance. There are no warnings for slurs or hate speech: anyone using them will receive an immediate permanent ban.
  • No Doxxing or Threats (Permanent Ban): Any real-life harassment, posting private personal information (doxxing), IP grabbing, DDoS threats, or harassing someone's family/school will get you permanently banned and reported. Keep all conflict strictly inside the game.
  • No Advertising: Do not post invite links to other Minecraft servers or commercial promotions.
  • Account Authentication & Responsibility: Both premium and offline-mode accounts are supported. When joining for the first time, follow the in-game GUI authentication prompt to secure your username. The in-game GUI prompt helps prevent automated spam bots from affecting the player count. Whatever actions happen under your username are your responsibility.

5. Map Resets & World Downloads

Map resets happen only when the community agrees to a fresh start. We always give a formal 45-day notice before any reset so you have plenty of time to finish your projects and take screenshots.

6. Reports & Appeals

How to handle issues with other players or bans:

  • Reporting Cheaters: If you catch someone using hacks (fly, speed, X-ray, killaura) or building lag machines, open a ticket on Discord with video clips or screenshots.
  • Ban Appeals: If you believe a ban was made by mistake, fill out our Ban Appeal Form or submit an appeal ticket on Discord. Appeals are reviewed directly by staff.
