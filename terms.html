By connecting to FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz:25565 or Bedrock port 19132) or participating in our official media channels, you explicitly acknowledge and agree to abide by the following terms, gameplay policies, and code of conduct.

Important Architecture Notice: FiestaSMP is designed around high-stakes survival. Players are expected to protect their own coordinates, secure their valuable chests, and maintain awareness of their surroundings in dangerous dimensions.

1. Gameplay & Combat Policy

FiestaSMP is built on unfiltered survival mechanics. The following rules dictate player interaction:

  • Permitted Mechanics: Raiding, griefing unprotected structures, theft from unlocked chests, and open-world PvP combat are permitted unless specified otherwise in designated spawn safe zones.
  • Combat Logging: Logging out or disconnecting while tagged in active combat carries an automatic death penalty and inventory drop.
  • Strictly Prohibited Exploits:
    • Use of hacked clients, automated cheat suites, X-ray texture packs, or speed hacks.
    • Intentional server crashing via lag machines, oversized chunk loaders, or entity spam.
    • Item duplication glitches of any kind. Any duped items will be wiped without compensation.

2. Physical Barter Economy & Trading Charter

FiestaSMP does not employ artificial virtual currency, scoreboard money, or pay-to-win server stores.

  • Physical Barter Only: All trade exchanges must happen using in-game physical items (e.g. diamonds, ancient debris, enchanted books, golden apples).
  • Trade Trust: Scamming or theft during non-spawn wilderness trades is considered a survival risk. Secure trading stalls are provided at Spawn Harbor for verified safe transactions.
  • No Real-World Trading (RWT): Selling server items, coordinates, or services for real-world currency or external compensation is strictly prohibited and results in a permanent ban.

3. Seasons & World Lifecycle

FiestaSMP operates on multi-year persistent worlds to give players sufficient time to build megastructures, expansive rail networks, and thriving community hubs.

World Resets: Major season resets occur only with community consensus or when major Minecraft version updates require fresh terrain generation. Players will always receive at least 30 days advance notice before any planned world archival.

4. Community Conduct & Harassment Policy

While PvP and raiding are permitted, toxicity outside of gameplay mechanics will not be tolerated:

  • Hate Speech: Racism, discrimination, severe harassment, dox threats, or hate speech in public chat or Discord will result in an immediate hardware and IP ban.
  • Staff Discretion: Staff members reserve the right to mute or remove disruptive individuals to preserve a safe community atmosphere.
  • Patience Requirement: When submitting ban appeals or inquiries, allow up to 48 hours for review. Spamming staff will result in an appeal rejection.

5. Administration & Disclaimer

FiestaSMP is an independent community server operated by the FiestaSMP Team. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Mojang Studios, Microsoft Corporation, or any other commercial entities.

For administrative questions or appeal inquiries, email fiestamg@murena.io or join our Discord server.
