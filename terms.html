02

Economy & trading

Pure barter model:

There is no virtual currency, /pay command, or server shop. All trading is strictly physical player-to-player barter.

Trade risks:

Item exchanges, clan alliances, and meetups occur at your own risk. Staff will not reimburse items lost to scamming, betrayal, or ambushes.

03

Seasons & resets

Lifecycle:

FiestaSMP operates on long-term seasonal lifecycles lasting roughly 1.5 to 3 years.

Wipes:

Full world and inventory resets happen exclusively at the launch of a new season and will be announced well in advance.

04

Community conduct

Doxxing, distribution of malicious software, network disruption attempts, and extreme hate speech will result in an immediate blacklist.

Attempting to circumvent network penalties using alternative accounts or proxies will result in automated bans across all connected profiles.

Discord & chat code

harassment, hate speech, bullying, being generally insufferable — not here. we’re a fiesta. act like it.
02 no spam
