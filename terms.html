Simple, fair rules for survival gameplay, land claims, trading, and chat conduct.

Welcome to FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz). To keep the game fun and fair for everyone, all players are expected to follow these rules.

Survival Notice: There is no spawn radius protection. The entire world is open survival with PvP and raiding enabled. To keep your base, chests, and builds safe from other players, make sure to claim your land.

1. Gameplay & Fair Play

We believe in a fair survival experience where skill and teamwork matter:

  • No Hacked Clients or Cheats: Using modified cheat clients (Fly, Speed, Killaura, Jesus), X-ray texture packs/mods, auto-clickers, freecam, seed crackers, or automated botting (like Baritone) results in an immediate permanent ban.
  • Allowed Modifications: Performance and visual client mods (Sodium, OptiFine, Iris), simple minimaps without player radar or cave mapping, and Litematica (printer/auto-place disabled) are allowed. If you are unsure about a mod, ask staff in Discord before using it.
  • No Lag Machines or Dupes: Intentionally creating lag machines, runaway redstone clocks, or exploiting item duplication glitches is strictly forbidden. Duplicated items will be removed without restoration.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting or closing your game while combat-tagged triggers an automatic combat death that drops your inventory.

2. Land Claims & Base Protection

Protect your builds and possessions using the claim system:

  • Claimed Land: Griefing, stealing, or damaging blocks inside claimed territory is strictly prohibited. If your claimed land is griefed, staff can inspect logs and roll back the damage.
  • Unclaimed Territory: Bases, farms, and chests left unclaimed in the wild are considered fair game for raiding. Always claim your land to secure your hard work.
  • Terrain Respect: Malicious terrain destruction around bases (such as massive lava casts or senseless cobblestone pillaring) is considered griefing and will be cleaned up.

3. Economy & Barter Rules

FiestaSMP runs on an authentic player-driven barter economy with zero pay-to-win mechanics:

  • Physical Currency: There are no virtual coins, scoreboard balances, or purchased rank shops. Trading is conducted using physical items like diamonds, gold, and Netherite.
  • Safe Trading: Use /trade <player> or trade directly in secure player shops. Dropping items on the ground in the wild carries risk.
  • No Real-Money Trading (RMT): Buying or selling in-game items, bases, currency, or services for real-world money is strictly prohibited and results in a permanent ban.

4. Chat & Community Conduct

We want FiestaSMP to remain welcoming for all players:

  • Respect Others: Harassment, hate speech, bullying, doxxing, personal threats, and severe toxicity will not be tolerated.
  • No Advertising: Do not promote other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, social media channels, or external products in public chat.
  • No Chat Spam: Keep chat readable. Avoid excessive capitalization, repeated message spamming, or automated bot broadcasts.
  • Account Responsibility: Any type of accounts are welcome on the server. You are responsible for all actions taken on your registered account name.

5. Season & Map Resets

Season and map resets happen whenever we feel like it, but a 45-day notice will always be given before any reset. After a season is shut down, its map archive will be uploaded so everyone can download and keep the world.

6. Appeals & Server Information

Need to report an issue or appeal a punishment?

  • Player & Bug Reports: If you spot someone breaking rules, cheating, or exploiting, open a support ticket on our Discord with screenshot or video evidence.
  • Ban Appeals: If you believe a punishment was issued in error, open an appeal ticket on Discord. Staff review tickets within 48 hours.

FiestaSMP is an independent multiplayer community and is not affiliated with Mojang AB or Microsoft Corporation. For administrative inquiries, email fiestamg@murena.io.
