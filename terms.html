Important Notice

Semi-No-Rules Direction (Effective Nov 10, 2026)

Effective November 10, 2026

FiestaSMP is shifting toward a semi-no-rules survival server. Griefing, stealing, base raiding, and open-world PvP are allowed. Land claims do not guarantee rollback or protection against base raiding.

Zero tolerance rules (cheats, doxxing, identity slurs, lag machines, duping) remain strictly enforced. Violating zero-tolerance rules results in an immediate permanent ban.

1. Cheats, Hacks & Client Mods

We do not tolerate hacked clients, unfair automation, or game-breaking modifications:

  • Strictly Banned (Permanent Ban): Modified hack clients (fly, speed, killaura, reach, auto-clickers), X-ray texture packs or mods, freecam, seed finders, and botting scripts like Baritone. Play with normal vanilla mechanics.
  • Allowed Mods: Client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris, Lithium, OptiFine), simple HUD mods, and basic minimaps (without entity radar, player tracking, or cave mapping). If you are unsure whether a mod is allowed, ask in Discord before logging in with it.
  • Lag Machines: Intentionally building redstone clocks, entity crammers, or chunk overload machines designed to lag or crash the server is strictly banned.
  • Duping Glitches: Any form of item duplication exploit is banned. Duped inventories are cleared and players involved are banned.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting or force-quitting during combat triggers an automatic death that drops your inventory where you stood.

2. Base Raiding, Griefing & Claims

How survival works under our semi-no-rules direction:

  • No Spawn Protection Radius: There is no protected spawn bubble. Outside of the immediate starter docks, the entire map is open survival.
  • Base Raiding & Griefing Allowed: Raiding bases, blowing through walls with TNT, looting chests, and taking builds is allowed. There is no rollback of raided blocks or looted items.
  • Stealing & Wild Looting: Any chests, containers, farms, or structures discovered in the wilderness are fair game to loot.
  • Free Barter & Trading: Unrestricted physical diamond and item trading between players.
  • Team Alliances & Clan Bases: You are free to group up, coordinate defenses, and build shared bases anywhere you choose.
  • Performance & Visual Mods: Client optimization mods (Sodium, Iris, Lithium, OptiFine) are encouraged.
  • Open World PvP: PvP combat is enabled everywhere outside spawn docks. Trust people at your own risk.
  • How to Keep Your Stuff Safe:
    • Travel far away from spawn (several thousand blocks out into the wilderness).
    • Build your base underground or disguise entrances with natural terrain.
    • Do not share your coordinates or show your F3 debug coordinates on streams or screenshots.

3. Diamond Trading & Player Shops

An honest survival economy without pay-to-win:

  • Barter & Diamond Economy: There are no virtual currency tokens, bought ranks, or virtual /eco money. Everything is traded with physical items, mainly diamonds and gold.
  • Trading Safely: Use /trade <player> for safe player-to-player trading with two-step confirmations, or set up chest shops at player bases. Dropping items on the ground in the wild is done at your own risk.
  • No Real-Money Trading (RMT): Selling or buying in-game items, accounts, or bases for real-world money is strictly prohibited and will get both parties banned.

4. Chat, Swearing & Real-Life Conduct

We want chat to feel natural without turning into real-life toxicity:

  • Casual Swearing & In-Game Warnings: Ordinary swearing used casually or out of frustration (such as "oh shit, I lost my axe" or "oh fuck") is allowed. The in-game chat monitor may issue automated warnings reminding you that swearing is discouraged, but casual profanity alone is not a punishable offense unless it goes too far.
  • Zero Tolerance for Slurs & Hate Speech (Permanent Ban): Identity-based slurs, racial or ethnic slurs, and severe harassment are strictly prohibited under zero tolerance. There are no warnings for slurs or hate speech: anyone using them will receive an immediate permanent ban.
  • No Doxxing or Threats (Permanent Ban): Any real-life harassment, posting private personal information (doxxing), IP grabbing, DDoS threats, or harassing someone's family/school will get you permanently banned and reported. Keep all conflict strictly inside the game.
  • No Advertising: Do not post invite links to other Minecraft servers or commercial promotions.
  • Account Authentication & Responsibility: Both premium and offline-mode accounts are supported. When joining for the first time, follow the in-game GUI authentication prompt to secure your username. The in-game GUI prompt helps prevent automated spam bots from affecting the player count. Whatever actions happen under your username are your responsibility.

5. Map Resets & World Downloads

Map resets happen only when the community agrees to a fresh start. We always give a formal 45-day notice before any reset so you have plenty of time to finish your projects and take screenshots.

6. Reports & Appeals

How to handle issues with other players or bans:

  • Reporting Cheaters: If you catch someone using hacks (fly, speed, X-ray, killaura) or building lag machines, open a ticket on Discord with video clips or screenshots.
  • Ban Appeals: If you believe a ban was made by mistake, submit an appeal ticket on Discord. Tickets are reviewed directly by staff.

FiestaSMP is an independent player-run Minecraft survival server and is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft. For server contact, email fiestamg@murena.io.
