Clear, fair rules for survival gameplay, land claims, diamond trading, and community conduct.

Welcome to FiestaSMP (fiesta.debarkak.xyz)! To keep the server fair, friendly, and competitive for everyone, all players must follow these rules.

Survival Notice: There is no spawn protection radius. The entire world is open survival with PvP and raiding enabled. To keep your base, chests, and builds safe from other players, make sure to claim your land!

1. Gameplay & Fair Play

Keep gameplay clean, fair, and fun for everyone:

  • No Hacked Clients or Cheats: Using modified cheat clients (Fly, Speed, Killaura, Jesus), X-ray texture packs/mods, auto-clickers, freecam, seed crackers, or automated botting (like Baritone) results in an immediate permanent ban.
  • Allowed Modifications: Performance and visual client mods (Sodium, OptiFine, Iris), simple minimaps without player radar or cave mapping, and Litematica (printer/auto-place disabled) are allowed. If you're ever unsure about a mod, ask staff in Discord before using it.
  • No Lag Machines or Dupes: Intentionally creating lag machines, runaway redstone clocks, or exploiting item duplication glitches is strictly forbidden. Duped items will be wiped, and players caught duping will be banned.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting or closing your game while combat-tagged triggers an automatic combat death that drops your inventory.

2. Land Claims & Base Protection

Protect your builds and loot using the claim system:

  • Claimed Land: Griefing, stealing, or destroying builds inside claimed land is strictly forbidden. If someone griefs your claim, staff can check the logs and roll back the damage.
  • Unclaimed Territory: Bases, farms, and chests left unclaimed out in the wild are considered fair game for raiding. Always claim your land to keep your hard work safe!
  • Terrain Respect: Lava casting someone's base or spamming cobblestone pillars around someone's build counts as griefing and will be cleaned up.

3. Economy & Barter Rules

FiestaSMP runs on a genuine player barter economy with zero pay-to-win:

  • Physical Currency: There are no virtual coins, scoreboard balances, or paid rank shops. Trading is done with physical items like diamonds, gold, and netherite.
  • Safe Trading: Use /trade <player> or trade directly in secure player shops. Dropping items on the ground in the wild is always at your own risk.
  • No Real-Money Trading (RMT): Buying or selling in-game items, bases, currency, or services for real-world money is strictly prohibited and results in a permanent ban.

4. Chat & Community Conduct

Keep chat chill and respect everyone playing:

  • Respect Others: Harassment, hate speech, bullying, doxxing, personal threats, and toxic behavior will get you muted or banned.
  • No Advertising: Do not promote other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, social media channels, or external products in public chat.
  • No Chat Spam: Keep chat readable. Don't spam messages, scream in all-caps, or use chat bots.
  • Account Responsibility: Any type of accounts are welcome on the server. You are responsible for all actions taken on your registered account name.

5. Season & Map Resets

Resets happen whenever we feel like doing a fresh start, with a 45-day notice given beforehand so you have plenty of time to finish builds and projects. After a season is shut down, world map archives are uploaded so everyone can download and keep the world.

6. Appeals & Server Information

Need to report an issue or appeal a punishment?

  • Player & Bug Reports: If you spot someone breaking rules, cheating, or exploiting, open a support ticket on our Discord with screenshot or video evidence.
  • Ban Appeals: If you believe a punishment was issued in error, open an appeal ticket on Discord. Staff review tickets within 48 hours.
